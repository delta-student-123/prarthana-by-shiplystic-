🔒 Strictly Necessary Cookies In-Depth

Strictly necessary cookies are automatically established upon page load. Without these, core platform capabilities cannot be delivered:

  • Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) Tokens: Protects your session from malicious third-party form manipulation while you enter personal devotee details.
  • Session Route Integrity: Ensures you do not lose your selected sanctum or puja date when navigating between multiple tabs.
  • DDoS & Traffic Filtering: Cloudflare edge nodes utilize cryptographic cookies to distinguish genuine human devotees from malicious automated scrapers.

📈 Performance & Diagnostic Telemetry

Performance cookies collect technical diagnostics without recording personal identities:

  • Measures load times across various Indian internet providers (Jio, Airtel, Vi, BSNL) to ensure 2-second page rendering nationwide.
  • Tracks unhandled JavaScript errors or checkout drop-offs so our development engineers can deploy rapid bug fixes.
  • Zero correlation with devotee phone numbers, names, or physical delivery addresses.
Section 05

💳 Payment Gateway Security & Fraud Defense

During checkout, our certified payment partners (Razorpay / Cashfree) establish temporary, highly encrypted security tokens. These tokens are mandated by Reserve Bank of India (RBI) regulations for:

  • Detecting fraudulent high-velocity transactions or stolen card testing.
  • Securing bank-issued OTP validation screens.
  • Confirming UPI callback webhooks to deliver instantaneous booking confirmations.
