⚠️ Non-Refundable Scenarios & Exceptions

To maintain fairness and protect the livelihood of officiating temple priests and logistics personnel, refunds cannot be approved under the following specific circumstances:

  • Successfully Completed Rituals: Once an authentic Sankalp has been solemnly recited before the deity and consecrated Prasad has been dispatched, the service is deemed fulfilled and non-refundable.
  • Incorrect or Unreachable Addresses: If a delivery fails because the devotee provided an invalid address, wrong pincode, or repeatedly ignored the courier delivery agent's phone calls, re-shipping fees will apply for re-attempt.
  • Metaphysical Expectations: As Vedic Pujas are acts of spiritual faith, refunds cannot be requested based on perceived personal astrological or metaphysical outcomes.
  • Late Reporting: Transit damage reported after more than 48 hours of delivery cannot be investigated with third-party air couriers.
