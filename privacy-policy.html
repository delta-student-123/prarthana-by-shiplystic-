All monetary transactions on Shiplystic Prarthana are processed through RBI-authorized, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (such as Razorpay, Cashfree, or PhonePe).

⚖️ Your Devotee Privacy Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and applicable data protection regulations, you hold sovereign control over your personal records:

  • Right to Access: You may request an itemized transcript of your past bookings, Sankalp details, and dispatch history.
  • Right to Rectification: If you notice an error in your name spelling, Gotra, or address, you can update it immediately prior to ritual scheduling.
  • Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten": Following successful Prasad delivery, you may request permanent removal of your contact numbers or delivery address from our active servers.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You may unsubscribe from celebratory festival updates or seasonal aarti reminders at any time by replying "STOP" on WhatsApp or emailing us.
