Section 01

🍪 What Are Cookies & Local Storage Technologies?

Cookies are small cryptographic text files placed on your computer, tablet, or smartphone by website servers when you navigate across our digital platforms (shiplystic.com).

Alongside standard HTTP cookies, Shiplystic utilizes modern HTML5 Local Storage and Session Storage. These technologies allow our web application to remember your active booking choices (such as your chosen temple sanctum, Gotra selection, or package quantity) as you navigate between pages without requiring you to re-enter your details repeatedly.

🛡️ Our Core Devotional Privacy Commitment

At Shiplystic Prarthana, we believe that spiritual seeking deserves complete sanctity. We employ cookies purely to facilitate a smooth, fast, and secure booking experience. We never use tracking cookies to build advertising dossiers or sell consumer records to third-party data brokerages.

Section 03

🔒 Strictly Necessary & Security Cookies

Strictly necessary cookies are indispensable for our website to operate securely and reliably. Without these technologies, fundamental services like adding a temple ritual to your cart, submitting your Gotra Sankalp, and processing digital transactions cannot function.

  • Session Authentication & Tokenization: Protects devotee booking submissions against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) and automated script injections.
  • Cart Persistence: Ensures your selected Puja dakshina and Prasad box remain safely stored in memory while you navigate temple guides.
  • Server Load Balancing: Distributes network traffic across secure cloud nodes so the site stays high-speed during auspicious peak festival days (e.g., Mahashivratri, Navratri, Janmashtami).

Note: Because these cookies are essential to platform security and functionality, they cannot be turned off through our cookie banner. They do not store any personally identifiable marketing information.

Section 05

📈 Performance & Diagnostic Analytics

We use privacy-preserving, aggregated telemetry to understand how devotees interact with Shiplystic Prarthana. These analytics assist our engineering team in optimizing site responsiveness:

  • Detecting slow page loading times across Indian 4G/5G mobile networks.
  • Monitoring 404 broken links or checkout interface errors to implement swift engineering fixes.
  • Analyzing aggregate temple popularity trends to ensure sufficient priest reservations are secured in advance.

All performance data is strictly anonymized, aggregated, and stripped of personal identifiable information (such as your name, mobile number, or Gotra).

Section 06

💳 Payment Gateway & Fraud Prevention Cookies

When you proceed to checkout, our RBI-authorized, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment processing partners (such as Razorpay or Cashfree) deploy temporary security cookies.

These cookies are strictly required by Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines to evaluate transaction risk, prevent card testing attacks, support multi-factor authentication (2FA / OTP verification), and ensure your UPI or net banking transmission remains 100% secure. Shiplystic itself never stores or has access to your banking cookies or card details.
