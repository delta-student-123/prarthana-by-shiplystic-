🪔 HOW PRARTHANA BEGAN

Bringing India's Revered Shrines Closer To Home

Shiplystic began as a Multi-Carrier Logistics Ecosystem — built on the promise to Ship, Track, and Scale deliveries across India by connecting multiple carrier partners into one seamless network. Prarthana grew out of that same promise, applied to something deeply personal: devotion.

We noticed how millions of devotees — living away from their hometown, family temple, or unable to travel — wished they could still receive their temple's sacred blessings. Prarthana was built to solve exactly that: you book, the temple prays, we deliver.

What We Are — and What We're Not

✕

What We Are NOT

  • • We are NOT a religious institution and do not perform any rituals or pujas ourselves.
  • • We do NOT claim ownership of any temple or alter traditional temple rituals.
  • • We NEVER send direct payment links, WhatsApp QR codes, or request OTPs.
✓

What We ARE

  • • We ARE a Multi-Carrier Logistics Ecosystem and booking platform facilitating authentic temple puja requests.
  • • Every prarthana is conducted directly by the participating temple's own priests according to their traditions.
  • • ₹501 of your payment is passed directly to the temple as your devotional sankalp donation.
  • • Fresh Maha Prasad is packed in tamper-proof packaging and delivered express to your home via our multi-carrier network.
