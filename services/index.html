5-Step Complete Process
01

🛕 Temple Puja Booking

Official Temple Ritual

Select your temple and share your devotee details. Your puja request is forwarded to the participating temple, where it is performed directly by the temple's own priests according to their traditions.

02

📜 Temple Donation Pass-Through

₹501 Direct Offering

₹501 of your payment is passed directly to the temple as your personal devotional offering — directly supporting the temple's daily rituals, prasad preparation, and shrine upkeep.

03

🧾 Proof & Receipt Confirmation

Official Documentation

Once your puja is complete, you receive an official donation receipt along with photo/video Sankalp proof shared from the temple confirming your prarthana was fulfilled.

04

📦 Prasad Collection & Secure Packaging

Tamper-Evident Pack

Our logistics team collects the fresh Prasad from the temple premises and packs it using hygienic, tamper-secure packaging specifically designed to preserve its sanctity in transit.

05

🚚 Multi-Carrier Doorstep Delivery

Live GPS Tracking

Powered by Shiplystic's Multi-Carrier Logistics Ecosystem, your blessed Prasad is routed through the fastest available carrier and delivered safely to your doorstep — anywhere in India.
