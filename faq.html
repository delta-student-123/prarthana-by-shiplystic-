Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our priest sankalp rituals, video recordings, prasad packaging, and shipping logistics.

How do I know my Name & Gotra Sankalp was actually recited? ▼
Every puja booking includes video proof. The temple priest explicitly recites your Primary Devotee Name, Gotra, and Rashi during the invocation ritual at the temple sanctum. A HD video clip is recorded and sent to your WhatsApp and email.
How is the Prasad packed to ensure hygiene and freshness during transit? ▼
All food items (dry fruits, mishri, pedhas) are sealed in food-grade vacuum pouches immediately after being offered to the deity. They are placed in rigid protective Shiplystic Prarthana boxes with moisture absorbers to guarantee 100% purity upon arrival.
How long does it take for Prasad delivery to reach my doorstep? ▼
Puja is typically performed within 24 to 48 hours of booking. Express delivery via Shiplystic logistics takes 2 to 4 business days across India and 5 to 7 days for international locations.
What if I do not know my family Gotra? ▼
If your family Gotra is unknown, you can mention "Kashyap Gotra" during booking. As per ancient Vedic scripture, Kashyap Gotra is universally applicable for divine Sankalps.
Can I track my order status in real time? ▼
Yes! Simply visit our Track Delivery page and enter your unique Order ID (e.g. PRARTHANA-892341) to monitor every step from priest assignment to final delivery.
