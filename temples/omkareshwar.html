Spiritual History & Significance

Shri Omkareshwar is one of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, situated on Mandhata island in the Narmada River, Khandwa district, Madhya Pradesh. Remarkably, the island itself is shaped naturally in the sacred Hindu symbol of 'OM' (🕉️) when viewed from above.

According to ancient Puranic legends, Vindhya mountain worshipped Lord Shiva here to gain spiritual supreme power. Pleased with Vindhya's devotion, Lord Shiva manifested as two Jyotirlingas: Omkareshwar (on the island) and Amareshwar / Mamleshwar (on the south bank). Visiting and offering Abhishekam at Omkareshwar bestows inner peace, wisdom, and liberation from ancestral karmas.

🌊 The Divine Power of Narmada Jal:

Every stone found in the Narmada River is considered a natural Shivlinga (*Narmadeshwar Shivlinga*). Water offered to Lord Omkareshwar is sanctified with cosmic vibration and carries immense healing and protective blessings for your household.
