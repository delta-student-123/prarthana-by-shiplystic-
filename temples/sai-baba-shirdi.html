Spiritual History & Miracles

Shri Saibaba of Shirdi is revered by millions of devotees across the globe as an incarnation of supreme divinity who preached the universal message of "Sabka Malik Ek" (One God governs all) and the twin virtues of Shraddha (Faith) and Saburi (Patience).

The sacred Dwarkamai Mosque in Shirdi houses the eternal fire (*Dhuni*) lit by Sai Baba over 100 years ago. The holy ash from this Dhuni, known as Udi, possesses miraculous healing power and brings relief to devotees suffering from illness, stress, and worldly troubles.

✨ The Healing Blessing of Shirdi Udi:

Applying Shirdi Udi on forehead or consuming a pinch with water invokes Sai Baba's instant protection and healing. Every Prasad box sent by Shiplystic includes authentic consecrated Udi from Shirdi.
